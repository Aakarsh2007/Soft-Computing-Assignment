Minimize f(x,y) = x² + y². Start A at (2,1) with velocity (1,0) and B at (1,0) at rest. B starts as swarm best because 1 < 5. Use w = 0.5, c₁ = c₂ = 1 and r₁ = r₂ = 0.5 in both coordinates.

Inertia 0.5 × (1,0) = (0.5,0)
Personal pull (0,0)
Social pull 0.5 × [(1,0) − (2,1)] = (−0.5,−0.5)
New state for A v = (0,−0.5), x = (2,0.5), f = 4.25

A improves its personal best from 5 to 4.25. B stays at (1,0) and keeps swarm best at 1.
